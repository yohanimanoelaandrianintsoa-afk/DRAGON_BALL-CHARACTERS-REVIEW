Column Descriptors:

name: Character's full name as shown in the series
ki: Base power level/fighting strength of the character
maxKi: Maximum power level achievable by the character
race: Character's species or racial background (e.g., Saiyan, Human, Namekian)
gender: Character's gender identity
description: Detailed character background and storyline information
image: URL to character's official image
affiliation: Character's primary group allegiance or association
